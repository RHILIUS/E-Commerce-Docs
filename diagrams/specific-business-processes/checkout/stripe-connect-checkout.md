# Stripe Connect proposal — superseded

The project now targets a resume/demo payment environment with a USD simulated
treasury, per-store wallets, seven-day settlement after delivery and mock withdrawals.

The maintained proposal is [Demo online checkout and USD wallets](demo-online-checkout.md),
with a [proposed wallet and settlement ERD](../../erd/demo-wallet-ledger.md).

The earlier connected-account onboarding and real transfer design is no longer
the proposed implementation. A future live-payments design would require separate review.
